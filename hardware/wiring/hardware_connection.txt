DeskDroid wiring reference

The authoritative, source-verified pin assignments and wiring assumptions are maintained in:

    docs/HARDWARE.md
    docs/pinout.md

Summary:

ESP32 to ESP8266 UART:
    ESP32 GPIO17 (TX) -> ESP8266 GPIO3 (RX)
    ESP32 GPIO16 (RX) <- ESP8266 GPIO1 (TX)
    Common ground required by the documented wiring assumption.

The firmware configures both sides for 115200 baud, 8N1. Power ratings, voltage compatibility, level shifting, relay isolation, and the complete schematic are not specified by this repository.
